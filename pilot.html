Pilot Projects

Explore a real document
challenge together.

ParancU Lab is looking for industrial partners to explore how evidence-oriented AI can improve access to information in complex technical and business documentation.

Our goal is to identify practical use cases, evaluate technical feasibility and develop solutions around measurable needs.

Potential applications

01

Manufacturing & Maintenance

Finding instructions, operating conditions and exceptions in technical manuals.

02

Engineering & R&D

Retrieving source-backed information from engineering specifications and technical reports.

03

Quality & Procedures

Checking whether documentation provides sufficient support for procedural questions.

04

Enterprise Knowledge

Making information in complex document collections easier to locate and inspect.

How a pilot works

  1. 01 — Define the challenge

    Identify a document collection, representative questions and clear evaluation criteria.

  2. 02 — Build and evaluate

    Test retrieval and evidence assessment against agreed references, documenting results and limitations.

  3. 03 — Review the opportunity

    Assess findings, technical feasibility and potential next steps.
