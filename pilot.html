Industrial pilots & collaboration

A real document problem.
A measured next step.

ParancU Lab is seeking industrial partners for jointly evaluated document-AI pilots. A pilot could help you understand where retrieval is useful, where it fails and whether further development is worth pursuing.

Potential applications

Where a collaboration could begin

Potential starting points for a pilot, not validated sector deployments.

01

Manufacturing & maintenance

Investigate retrieval of instructions, conditions and exceptions from technical manuals.

A pilot would assess missed evidence and critical omissions on representative documents.

02

Engineering & R&D

Explore source-backed facts and comparisons in technical reports.

A pilot would check whether facts remain tied to the correct sources and conditions.

03

Quality & procedures

Study whether retrieved passages support procedural questions and reveal information gaps.

Independent review would distinguish sufficient, ambiguous and absent information.

04

Internal knowledge access

Evaluate inspectable retrieval over an agreed, bounded collection of documents.

Document access and processing arrangements would be agreed before material is shared.

Proposed pilot process

Agree the question.
Measure the evidence.

  1. 01 / SCOPE

    Define the task

    Agree document access, data permissions, representative questions and success criteria.

  2. 02 / EVALUATE

    Build a baseline

    Review reference evidence and assess retrieval, sufficiency, failure cases and operating costs separately.

  3. 03 / DECIDE

    Review the findings

    Document limitations and human-review needs, then decide whether further development is justified.

What a pilot could deliver

  • Baseline measurements: an agreed starting point for the selected document tasks.
  • Retrieval findings: where relevant passages supply the requested evidence and where gaps remain.
  • Documented failure cases: missed evidence, incomplete support and cases needing human review.
  • A development assessment: findings to inform whether to continue, narrow the scope or stop.

These are proposed evaluation outputs to agree together, not guaranteed operational improvements or commercial results.

Planned enterprise direction

Explore cloud & API
integration.

One direction is server-side document preparation with versioned corpora or retrieval services, optionally connected to evidence-sufficiency decisions and generated answers.

This is a planned direction. Authentication, tenancy, hosting, data residency, retention and service commitments would need to be specified and validated. An enterprise API is not offered as a released service here.
