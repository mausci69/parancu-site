Technology

From documents
to evidence.

ParancU Lab develops technologies to retrieve information from documents while keeping the original sources visible and inspectable.

Our approach distinguishes finding relevant passages from determining whether they contain enough information to answer a question.

How it works

  1. 01 / PREPARE

    Prepare

    Create searchable document passages with retrieval representations.

  2. 02 / RETRIEVE

    Retrieve

    Combine multilingual semantic matching and lexical signals to find potential evidence.

  3. 03 / EVALUATE

    Evaluate

    Inspect passages; investigate whether evidence is sufficient, incomplete or unrelated.

Sufficiency evaluation is experimental, not integrated into ParancU iOS or a production pipeline.

Beyond similarity

A document can discuss the right topic without containing the fact being requested.

ParancU 2.0 and Decision Lab experimentally explore factual representations, richer question spaces and evidence sufficiency.
