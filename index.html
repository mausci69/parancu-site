Built around a practical question

Does the document contain
what you need to know?

Technical documents can be relevant without containing the required fact. For example, a maintenance passage may describe a pump without stating its pressure limit for the operating condition in question. ParancU Lab keeps source evidence visible and studies that distinction before relying on a generated answer.
