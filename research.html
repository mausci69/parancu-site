Research & Development

Useful evidence.
Testable claims.

Research at ParancU Lab examines how document evidence is represented, retrieved and judged. Implementations, experimental observations and unresolved questions are reported separately.

A passage can mention the right people, equipment or process yet leave out the fact a question needs. Matching factual evidence means preserving who did what, under which conditions; assessing sufficiency means checking whether all the information needed to answer is present. Similar wording alone does not settle either question.

Method before headline numbers

Preserve the experiment.
Report what failed.

Freeze and compare

Named studies preserve inputs, configurations and raw outputs, and compare against baselines. Some use repeated seeds or trials.

Separate evidence levels

Deterministic tests check software behaviour. Model judgments, synthetic labels and human-reviewed references have different evidential weight.

Keep the limitations

Report dataset size, annotation provenance, operational errors and negative findings. Related evidence sets are not independent trials.

Selected recorded findings

Results in their actual scope.

These studies measure different tasks and cannot be combined into a Lab-wide accuracy score. The newer studies are research observations, not product performance promises.

Selected experiments · snapshot of recorded research
Study Recorded observation Limits & interpretation
ParancU 2.0 · factual accessibility Experimentally verified observation

On 12 claim contrasts, token-level MaxSim correctly ordered 10/12 pairs versus 8/12 for global E5 cosine. Role inversions were 1/3 versus 0/3.

 Small, related sample. This is pair ordering, not answer accuracy. General factual advantage remains inconclusive.
ParancU 2.0 · learned matching Inconclusive

On 64 synthetic test contrasts across four test families, two seeds achieved 70.3% and 73.4% pair ordering versus 67.2% for global E5.

 Both failed the predefined success criterion. No demonstrated real-document generalization.
ParancU 2.0 · source binding Negative finding

Both seeds remained at 50% pair accuracy and 0/4 groups correctly following both source assignments in a new test family.

 A narrow diagnostic failure, not proof that source attribution is generally impossible.
Decision Lab · real retrieval Experimentally verified observation

Ten questions on one 75-chunk QA corpus: sufficiency-proxy checks passed on 6/9 answerable questions, with 6/10 correct overall outcomes.

 One diagnosis execution error; sentence-provenance coverage is a conservative proxy, not semantic answer quality.
Decision Lab · requirement verification Experimentally verified observation

On 30 synthetic question families and 90 correlated evidence sets, 85/90 classifications matched frozen author labels.

 Four false COMPLETE and one false rejection. Labels were assistant-authored/reviewed, not independently human-validated; some scope was ambiguous.
Agent Lab · multi-evidence Experimentally verified observation

A historical ten-question run passed 7/10 final-outcome checks; 1/4 intrinsically multi-evidence cases used multiple chunks.

 One prepared QA corpus. Outcome checks are not independent answer-accuracy validation or current production measurements.

Selected newer findings are summarized from reviewed research records; not all underlying artifacts are publicly published. No independently validated product-wide reliability score is established.

The paper reports local Top-5 evidence-containment maxima of 85% for Question Answering, 82% for Sicilia Normanna and 78% for reconstructed Multiple Sclerosis.

Settings were selected on the evaluated benchmark grids. These are descriptive maxima, not held-out performance estimates. Python/mobile numerical parity was not established. These figures belong to that research snapshot, not ParancU 2.0 or Agent Lab.

Open questions

What remains unresolved

Fidelity and sufficiency

Extraction can lose conditions, misattribute arguments, leave references unresolved or duplicate meaning. Verification can confuse ownership with employment, alternatives with combinations, or affirmative truth with answerability.

Validation and integration

Broader independently reviewed datasets, multilingual evaluation and deployment validation remain needed. A recorded cloud integration attempt stopped before inference; synthetic tests do not establish end-to-end semantic behaviour.

A hybrid-verifier study on 49 cases produced 33 validation errors, two uncertain outcomes and 14 decisive outputs, including two false COMPLETE judgments. Fewer counted false positives did not demonstrate correction when previous failures became errors.
