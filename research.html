ParancU 2.0 · In development

Factual representation & interrogative space

Research explores extracting facts from document chunks and generating meaningful questions. This “interrogative space”—the questions a passage can answer—aims to retrieve answer-bearing evidence beyond thematic similarity.

Decision Lab · In development

Evidence sufficiency

Investigate whether passages provide sufficient, complementary evidence, what information is missing and how to retrieve more.

ParancU 2.0 focuses on finding answer-bearing evidence; Decision Lab investigates whether that evidence is sufficient. Their integration is currently under development.

Research snapshot · 20 October 2026

Implemented components include source-linked sentence representations, multiple-question retrieval and experimental evidence-selection logic.

Baseline comparisons record successes and failures. Conditions, attribution and missing information remain difficult. Tests and model judgments do not establish general reliability; independent evaluation remains necessary.

Looking ahead

Toward cloud & API integration.

Planned cloud/API integration would connect document preparation, retrieval and evidence assessment.

Document confidentiality is a design requirement, not a guarantee. Processing, access and retention arrangements require definition and validation.
